Website Redesign — Design Concepts

Two distinct design directions for the new Apple Hospitals website, covering Apple Saraswati Multispeciality Hospital, Apple Cancer Institute and Apple Diagnostics. Each concept includes a homepage, a Find a Doctor page and a Diagnostics & Health Packages page, and works on desktop, tablet and mobile.

SAMPLE 1

Premium Healthcare Corporate

Structured, institutional and trustworthy — inspired by premium hospital brands.

  • Clean white layout with deep navy, healthcare blue and controlled red accents
  • Prominent doctor search panel and clear department grid
  • Professional doctor cards, packages and a dedicated Cancer Institute section
  • Single, strong "Book Appointment" action ready for a booking system or third-party link
No embedded booking form — the appointment button shows where a future booking feature will connect.

SAMPLE 2

Modern Patient-Centric Experience

Immersive, warm and visually engaging — designed around what patients need to do.

  • Full-width hero imagery with a floating navigation bar
  • Interactive appointment-booking widget in the homepage hero
  • "How can we help?" shortcuts, doctor carousel and diagnostics showcase
  • Mobile action dock for Call, Doctors and Book on phones
The booking widget is a working front-end demonstration — no data is sent or stored, and no real appointment is created.
