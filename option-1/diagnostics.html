Apple Diagnostics

Accurate diagnosis, the first step to the right treatment

Apple Diagnostics is part of Apple Hospitals and Research Institute Limited, providing imaging and laboratory services for patients of Apple Saraswati Multispeciality Hospital, Apple Cancer Institute and referring doctors.
